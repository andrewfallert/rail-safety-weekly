Refund and Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: July 1, 2026 | Last Updated: July 1, 2026

This is a plain-language policy for Rail Safety Weekly LLC, provided for transparency. It is not a substitute for legal advice.

This Refund and Cancellation Policy (“Policy”) governs the cancellation of subscriptions to, and the availability of refunds for, the services offered by Rail Safety Weekly LLC (the “Company”). This Policy is incorporated into and forms part of the Company’s Terms of Service.

1. Subscription Terms

1.1  The Briefing Plan ($249.00 per month) and the Tailored Program ($449.00 per month) are billed monthly in advance and renew automatically for successive monthly terms until canceled.

2. Cancellation

2.1  You may cancel your Subscription at any time through your account or by written request to support@railsafetyweekly.com. Cancellation terminates the automatic renewal of your Subscription and prevents further charges.

3. Effect of Cancellation

3.1  Upon cancellation, access to the Service shall continue through the end of the then-current paid billing period, after which the Subscription shall terminate.

4. No Refunds

4.1  ALL FEES ARE NON-REFUNDABLE. THE COMPANY DOES NOT PROVIDE REFUNDS OR CREDITS FOR ANY PARTIAL BILLING PERIOD, OR FOR ANY BILLING PERIOD THAT HAS ALREADY COMMENCED OR BEEN BILLED. CANCELLATION HALTS FUTURE CHARGES ONLY.

5. Failed Payments

5.1  In the event a payment fails, the Company may suspend access to the Service until a valid payment method is provided. Repeated failed payments may result in termination of the Subscription.

6. Price Changes

6.1  The Company may modify Plan fees upon not less than thirty (30) days’ prior notice. You may cancel prior to the effective date of any fee increase.

7. Billing Errors

7.1  If you believe you have been charged in error, you must notify the Company within thirty (30) days of the charge. The Company shall review and, where a genuine error is confirmed, correct such charge.

8. Contact

8.1  Billing inquiries may be directed to support@railsafetyweekly.com.
